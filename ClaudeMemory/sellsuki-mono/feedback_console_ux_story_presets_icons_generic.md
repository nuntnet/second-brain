When nunt reviewed the settings redesign (prototype v1→v9, 2026-10-31), they corrected the following.

- **"ครบแต่ท่วมท้น"**: complete but overwhelming. A page that stacks every feature as boxes scares a first-time admin. Order the content as a story (a guided wizard, one decision per screen), and keep the full view collapsed to one-line summaries with advanced settings tucked away. Grouping and links between pages are what nunt liked; keep those.
- **"ควรมี preset หรือ suggestion ไม่ใช่การพิมพ์ free text"**: offer presets or suggestions instead of free text. Persona and channel setup should be picks: tone cards with sample replies, chips, a live preview. Typing is an optional override.
- **"ไม่ใช่ label ตรงๆ ควรมี icon mouse hover แสดง"**: per-message and status details are muted icons whose tooltip shows on hover or focus, not text labels. Use colour only for failures and items that need attention.
- **"ขายประกัน … ทำให้ระบบดูแคบ"**: the platform serves HR chatbots, e-commerce, car repair and body-and-paint claims, contract manufacturing, clinics and appointments. Demos, template names and defaults must never assume insurance, or any single industry. Use generic templates (ขาย / รับเรื่องและติดตามงาน / นัดหมาย / สั่งผลิต / ช่วยเหลือภายใน) and sample businesses.
- nunt asks to be shown how settings appear on the display side (Customers, board, inbox), not only the settings screen.

**Why:** v1 was rejected as incomplete and wrong, and v3 as overwhelming. Each item above is nunt's own correction.
**How to apply:** do this for any console screen design or prototype. Check the design against these rules before showing it to nunt. Related: [[feedback_selfexplaining_ux]], [[feedback_design_handoff_full_fidelity]].
